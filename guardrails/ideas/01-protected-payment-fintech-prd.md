# Naitrust Protected Payment Fintech PRD

Status: Product baseline  
Product: Naitrust  
Audience: Individuals and businesses  
Primary market: Nigeria

## Product vision

Naitrust is a trusted payment workflow for transactions where sending money directly creates avoidable risk. It combines verified participants, clear agreement terms, protected funding through regulated payment partners, evidence, human approvals and controlled settlement in one Deal Room.

Naitrust is not a bank and must not claim to directly hold customer funds. Licensed or appropriately regulated providers perform custody, collection, settlement, refunds and other regulated money movement.

## Problem

Individuals and businesses routinely pay unfamiliar sellers, vendors, contractors and service providers through informal chats and direct transfers. The agreement, identity, evidence and payment are disconnected. When delivery fails or work is disputed, neither side has a reliable shared record or a controlled release process.

## Product promise

**Agree the terms. Protect the payment. Release on proof.**

Naitrust should make five things unambiguous:

1. Who is paying and who will receive money.
2. What each party agreed to provide.
3. How much is protected and which provider holds it.
4. What evidence or approval permits release.
5. What happens if the transaction is cancelled or disputed.

## Core users

### Individuals

- Customers paying deposits for services.
- People buying vehicles or other high-value goods.
- Customers paying freelancers, artisans or contractors.

### Businesses

- SMEs paying suppliers and vendors.

- Teams managing procurement and contractor milestones.
- Businesses requiring approval roles and auditable records.

## Core product: Protected Deal

A Protected Deal contains:

- Verified payer and payee.
- Confirmed beneficiary details.
- Transaction purpose and category.
- Amount, currency, fees and provider disclosures.
- Deliverables, deadline and release conditions.
- Single-release or supported milestone schedule.
- Messages, invoices, documents and evidence.
- Funding, approval, dispute, refund and settlement timeline.
- Immutable audit events.

## Primary journey

1. The creator chooses individual or business use and describes the transaction.
2. Naitrust records the amount, terms, release conditions and evidence requirements.
3. The creator invites the other party.
4. Both parties complete required identity, business and deal-specific checks.
5. The payer funds through a supported regulated payment provider.
6. The payee completes the obligation and submits relevant evidence.
7. The payer approves release, requests clarification or reports an issue.
8. The provider settles, refunds or keeps funds restricted according to an authorised instruction.
9. Naitrust stores receipts, decisions and the final transaction status.

## Success measures

- Protected Deals created and accepted.
- Funding completion rate.
- Successful release rate.
- Median time from obligation completion to release.
- Dispute rate and median resolution time.
- Refund completion rate.
- Verification completion rate.
- Support contacts per funded deal.
- Repeated use by individuals and businesses.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- Never state that Naitrust directly holds funds unless its regulatory position changes and legal approval is recorded.
- Never release based solely on frontend state.
- Never change beneficiary details after funding without a controlled re-verification and approval process.
- Never hide fees, release rules, provider identity or refund treatment.
- Never describe verification as a guarantee.
- Never allow an AI system to make an irreversible release or dispute decision without authorised controls.
