# Naitrust Protected Commerce Positioning

Last updated: 16 August 2026

## The problem

Nigerian individuals and businesses struggle to source confidently from unfamiliar local and international suppliers. Listings may be difficult to understand, supplier identity can be unclear, catalogue prices rarely show the complete cost to Nigeria, and product, inspection, logistics, customs, payment, and delivery updates are scattered across chats and agents.

Businesses face the same problem as individual buyers. They need reliable stock, machinery, packaging, private-label production, inspection, and shipping, while also needing a trusted way to showcase and sell their own products.

## The Naitrust solution

Naitrust brings protected commerce into one journey:

1. Discover verified suppliers and products in China or Nigeria.
2. Browse translated listings and supplier showcases in clear English.
3. Request a confirmed quote showing the landed cost and delivery estimate.
4. Pay in NGN or USD through the approved partner structure.
5. Track preparation, optional inspection, export, customs, local delivery, evidence, and release in one Order Room.

Business accounts can both source and sell. They use the same Market, Cart, Quotes, Orders, and Agents journey as individuals, while also publishing a Showcase, managing Products, fulfilling customer orders, and receiving earnings.

## Core value proposition

**Find the right supplier. Know the complete cost. Protect the order. Track it home.**

## Sourcing agents

Naitrust may suggest a verified independent agent when an order could benefit from market sourcing, a factory visit, product inspection, negotiation assistance, or evidence collection. The suggestion is optional. The customer reviews the agent, scope, deadline, and separate fee before hiring. Agents never control supplier purchase funds.

## Required claim boundaries

- Do not call a curated or mocked supplier integration live.
- Do not imply that verification guarantees quality, honesty, delivery, or customs outcomes.
- Do not claim Naitrust directly holds customer funds.
- Do not present an AI suggestion as an automatic decision or endorsement.
- Do not imply agents are Naitrust employees unless that employment relationship exists.
