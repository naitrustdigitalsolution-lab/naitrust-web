Last updated: 10 August 2026

## 1. The Problem

Buying from or doing business with someone new can feel risky.

Buyers worry about paying and receiving the wrong item, a damaged product, incomplete work, or nothing at all. Sellers and service providers worry about dishonest complaints, delayed payments, fake payment claims, and releasing goods or completing work without a reliable record.

Most conversations happen across WhatsApp, Instagram, phone calls, receipts, bank alerts, and delivery messages. When something goes wrong, the agreement, payment, evidence, and delivery information are scattered across different places. Both sides may remember the deal differently, and resolving the issue becomes difficult.

Ordinary transfers move money, but they do not capture the full agreement or provide a shared process for delivery confirmation, evidence, payment release, and disputes.

## 2. The Naitrust Solution

Naitrust helps buyers, sellers, businesses, and service providers create a clear, trackable agreement before an important payment moves.

With a Naitrust Protected Deal, both parties can keep the following information together in one Deal Room:

- verified participant details;
- agreed product or service information;
- payment amount and release conditions;
- delivery or completion milestones;
- seller and buyer evidence;
- delivery confirmation;
- messages and supporting documents;
- issue reports and dispute records.

Protected payments move through an approved regulated payment partner. Naitrust provides the technology that connects identity, agreements, evidence, delivery, and payment instructions. Naitrust does not transport goods, provide parcel insurance, or present itself as a bank.

## 20. Marketing Claims to Avoid

Do not publish claims such as:

- “Naitrust guarantees every delivery.”
- “You can never lose money with Naitrust.”
- “Naitrust insures every parcel.”
- “Naitrust is a bank.”
- “Naitrust directly holds your money.”
- “Every verified seller is completely trustworthy.”
- “All disputes are resolved within 24 hours.”
- “The buyer is always right” or “the seller is always protected.”

Prefer accurate language such as:

- “Naitrust helps both parties create a clear and trackable deal record.”
- “Protected payments move through an approved regulated payment partner.”
- “Naitrust can freeze automatic payment release when a supported delivery issue and relevant evidence are reported.”
- “Verification helps confirm that you are dealing with the intended participant.”

## 21. Brand Voice

Naitrust’s voice should be:

- clear rather than technical;
- confident without making guarantees;
- fair to both buyers and sellers;
- practical and locally understandable;
- calm when discussing risk;
- specific about what the product does and does not cover.

Avoid fear-based messaging that makes every seller, buyer, or online transaction sound fraudulent. Naitrust exists to make legitimate deals clearer and easier to trust.
