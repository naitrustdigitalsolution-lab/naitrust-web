# Naitrust Protected Payments Positioning

## The problem

People and businesses often send money before they can verify the other party, document the agreement or confirm that an obligation was completed. Direct transfers separate the payment from the terms, evidence and resolution process.

## The Naitrust solution

Naitrust connects the complete protected-payment journey:

1. Define the transaction and release conditions.
2. Verify the parties and beneficiary.
3. Fund through regulated payment rails.
4. Keep money protected under the agreed workflow.
5. Approve release, request a refund or report an issue with evidence.

## Core value proposition

**Agree the terms. Protect the payment. Release on proof.**

## Short brand description

Naitrust gives individuals and businesses a trusted way to create clear agreements, protect payments through regulated providers and control release with evidence and approval.

## Required claim boundaries

- Do not claim Naitrust directly holds customer funds.
- Do not call Naitrust a bank, licensed escrow trustee, insurer or guarantor.
- Describe the experience as escrow-style unless legal approval permits a stronger claim.
- Do not imply verification guarantees performance or prevents fraud.
- Do not imply funding, release or refund succeeded before provider confirmation.
- Identify mocked, pilot and planned capabilities accurately.
