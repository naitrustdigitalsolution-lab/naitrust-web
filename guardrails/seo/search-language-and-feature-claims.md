# Naitrust SEO Search Language and Feature Claims

Last updated: 16 August 2026

## Purpose

This file records useful search language and prevents search optimisation from turning a planned, partial, mocked, or nonexistent capability into a public product claim.

Search demand is not proof that Naitrust provides a feature. A phrase may be saved here for research without being added to page titles, descriptions, structured data, AI summaries, social previews, or visible product copy.

## Required availability check

Before publishing any feature phrase, confirm and record all of the following:

1. The customer-facing feature exists and is usable in the intended environment.
2. Backend enforcement and payment-partner support exist where money moves.
3. The feature is not only a mock, disabled option, design, local state, or future roadmap item.
4. Compliance and legal wording has been approved where the phrase implies custody, protection, verification, security, credit, insurance, or regulated activity.
5. The public wording describes the feature accurately and does not promise a guaranteed outcome.

If any check fails, keep the phrase in the research list and label the feature **Planned**, **Coming soon**, **Pilot**, or **Not currently available** wherever it is mentioned publicly.

## Product hierarchy

Even when a secondary feature is available, Naitrust's public hierarchy remains:

1. Discover a product, service, supplier, or sourcing agent in China or Nigeria.
2. Review translated listings, supplier verification, showcases, and Trust Profiles.
3. Request and approve a confirmed landed-cost quote.
4. Pay through a protected order and track inspection, logistics, customs, delivery, evidence, and release.

Legacy transfers, bill payment, airtime, rewards, virtual cards, and similar utilities remain secondary and outside the primary navigation story.

## SEO integrity rules

- Do not use hidden text, doorway pages, repetitive keyword blocks, or misleading structured data.
- Do not add a term only because it receives search traffic when the page does not answer that search intent.
- Meta keywords are a vocabulary record, not evidence that major search engines will rank the page.
- Prefer a useful public page with accurate, natural language for every important search theme.
- Keep “Protected Deal” as the product name. “Protected transaction” may be used descriptively.
- “Escrow” may be used only for carefully qualified comparison intent. Never describe Naitrust as an escrow provider unless the legal and regulated structure explicitly supports that claim.
- “Safe” and “secure” must not imply risk-free, guaranteed, fraud-proof, or irreversible protection.
- AI discovery files must distinguish currently available capabilities from pilots and planned features.

## Change control

Any new SEO feature cluster must be reviewed against this file and the main product guardrails. When code, operational support, and marketing disagree, use the narrowest truthful description until the discrepancy is resolved.
